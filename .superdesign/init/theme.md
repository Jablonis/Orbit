# Orbit theme

Orbit is dark, restrained, data-led, and operational. Geist is the control/data
face; a system editorial serif is reserved for Brief and reflection statements.

Key semantic tokens from `src/app/globals.css`:

- canvas/surfaces: `--canvas`, `--surface-1`, `--surface-2`,
  `--surface-overlay`, `--surface-scrim`
- text: `--text-primary`, `--text-secondary`, `--text-tertiary`
- borders: `--border-subtle`, `--border-strong`, `--border-focus`
- status: `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, `--accent-info`
- accents/data: `--accent-primary`, `--accent-focus`, `--accent-highlight`,
  `--data-series-1` through `--data-series-5`
- geometry: `--radius-panel`, `--radius-row`, `--radius-control`,
  `--control-height`, `--space-card`
- motion: `--ease-orbit`, `--duration-micro`, `--duration-state`

Surfaces are opaque and continuous. Blur belongs only to navigation and
overlays. Focus is always visible in lime. Reduced-motion removes nonessential
animation. Important meaning is never color-only.
